How this works

1Tap Start and choose your district and your name. If your name is not on the list, you can type it.
2Read each situation and pick the best action. Only one answer is best.
3You have 30 minutes. The test submits itself when time is up.
4Tap Submit. You will see your score, your skill profile, and the correct answers.

Before you begin

  • Work on your own. This is not graded as pass or fail. It shows where the training can help you most.
  • You can go back to any item and change your answer before you submit.
  • Do not refresh the page or press Home while answering, or your answers will be lost.
  • You can take this posttest only once. The same name and district cannot submit twice.
